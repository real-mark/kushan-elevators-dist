The company

Kushan Elevators

Kushan Elevators is a trusted engineering firm operating from Mutungo, Kampala. The company specializes in the supply, setup, and robust lifecycle maintenance of modern passenger and cargo lift systems across Uganda. Known for its hands-on approach, the company serves as a vital service provider to building owners who prioritize consistent up-time and immediate engineering response over digital storefronts.
